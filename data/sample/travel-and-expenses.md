# Northwind Tech — Travel and Expense Policy

> Sample document for the DocSense demo. Northwind Tech is a fictional company.

## Booking travel
All business travel must be booked through the company travel desk at least 7 days before departure.
Domestic flights must be booked in economy class. Business class is allowed only for international flights longer than 8 hours, with VP approval.
Train travel on routes under 500 km is preferred over flights.

## Hotel limits
The nightly hotel limit is ₹8,000 in metro cities (Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune) and ₹5,000 in other cities.
Stays above the limit need written approval from the employee's manager before booking.

## Daily allowance
Employees on domestic travel receive a daily meal allowance of ₹1,500. On international travel the daily allowance is USD 60.
Alcohol is never reimbursable.

## Submitting expenses
Expense claims must be submitted in the finance portal within 30 days of the trip ending. Claims submitted later than 60 days are rejected.
Every expense above ₹500 needs an itemised receipt. Approved claims are reimbursed with the next monthly salary.

## Local conveyance
Cab rides for client visits are reimbursed at actual cost. Use of a personal vehicle is reimbursed at ₹12 per km for cars and ₹5 per km for two-wheelers.
