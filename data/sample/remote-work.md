# Northwind Tech — Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

> Sample document for the DocSense demo. Northwind Tech is a fictional company.

## Hybrid schedule
Employees in hybrid roles work from the office at least 2 days per week. Tuesdays and Thursdays are team anchor days.
Fully remote roles are approved by the department head and reviewed every 12 months.

## Working from another city
Employees may work from another city in India for up to 30 days per year, with their manager's approval.
Working from outside India is not allowed without prior approval from HR and the legal team, because of tax and compliance rules.

## Home office support
New hybrid and remote employees receive a one-time home office allowance of ₹15,000 for a chair, desk or monitor.
A monthly internet allowance of ₹1,000 is paid with salary.

## Core hours
Core collaboration hours are 11:00 to 16:00 IST. Meetings should not be scheduled outside core hours unless all attendees agree.

## Equipment return
Employees leaving the company must return their laptop and accessories within 7 days of their last working day.
