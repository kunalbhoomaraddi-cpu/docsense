# Northwind Tech — Leave Policy

> Sample document for the DocSense demo. Northwind Tech is a fictional company.

## Annual leave
Full-time employees earn 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, credited at 2 days per month.
Up to 10 unused annual leave days can be carried forward to the next year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 March.
Annual leave requests of 3 days or more must be submitted at least 14 days in advance through the HR portal.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward.
A medical certificate is required for sick leave longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Parental leave
Birthing parents receive 26 weeks of fully paid maternity leave.
Non-birthing parents receive 4 weeks of paid paternity leave, which must be used within 6 months of the child's birth or adoption.
Adoptive parents of a child under 3 months old receive 12 weeks of paid leave.

## Public holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays per year. The holiday list for each office is published in December for the following year.
Employees who work on a public holiday at their manager's request get one compensatory day off, to be used within 30 days.

## Leave without pay
Leave without pay of up to 3 months may be approved by the department head after all paid leave is used.
