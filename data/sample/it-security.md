# Northwind Tech — IT Security Policy

> Sample document for the DocSense demo. Northwind Tech is a fictional company.

## Passwords and sign-in
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not be reused across the last 5 passwords.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, VPN, the HR portal and all production systems.
Passwords must never be shared, including with IT support staff.

## Devices
Company laptops are encrypted with full-disk encryption and must lock automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
Personal devices may access company email only through the approved mobile management app.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to the security team within 1 hour at security@northwind.example.

## Software
Only software from the internal software catalogue may be installed on company laptops. Requests for new software go through an IT ticket and are reviewed within 3 working days.
Security patches are installed automatically and laptops must be restarted within 48 hours of a patch being released.

## Data handling
Customer data is classified as Confidential and must not be stored on personal devices or personal cloud storage.
Confidential files may be shared outside the company only through the approved file-transfer tool, with links that expire within 7 days.

## Phishing
Suspicious emails should be reported with the "Report phishing" button in the mail client. Employees who report a simulated phishing email within 10 minutes are recognised in the monthly security newsletter.
