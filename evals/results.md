## Refusal threshold (hybrid, 600 chars)

Confidence = average of (a) the retriever's relevance score and (b) query coverage: how much of the question's meaning appears in the best passage. Below the threshold, DocSense says it couldn't find the answer instead of guessing. Higher threshold = fewer made-up answers, but more real questions refused.

| Threshold | Answerable questions answered | Unanswerable questions refused |
|---|---|---|
| 0.10 | 100% | 30% |
| 0.14 | 100% | 30% |
| 0.18 | 100% | 30% |
| 0.22 | 100% | 30% |
| 0.26 | 97% | 40% |
| 0.30 | 97% | 40% |
| 0.34 | 94% | 70% |
| 0.38 ← chosen | 91% | 100% |
| 0.42 | 91% | 100% |
| 0.46 | 79% | 100% |
| 0.50 | 79% | 100% |
| 0.54 | 73% | 100% |
| 0.58 | 70% | 100% |

Best balanced threshold on this set: **0.38** (answers 91%, refuses 100%). The app uses `min_confidence = 0.38`.

## Failure cases (extractive answerer)

- **How early must I apply for a week of vacation?** — expected `14 days`, got: _I couldn't find this in the documents._
- **How much paternity leave do fathers get?** — expected `4 weeks`, got: _Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. [1]_
- **What should I do if I lose my laptop?** — expected `1 hour`, got: _I couldn't find this in the documents._
- **Can I work from Goa for a few weeks?** — expected `30 days`, got: _Employees in hybrid roles work from the office at least 2 days per week. [1]_
- **Can I work remotely from Dubai?** — expected `not allowed`, got: _I couldn't find this in the documents._
